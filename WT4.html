Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery and Procurement - Work Term Report Winter 2026

Welcome to my website! My name is Mohammad Sameer and I'm a 4th year Software Engineering student at the University of Guelph. This blog will shed light on my 4 month co-op work term as a Junior Technical Analyst at the Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery and Procurement (MPBSDP).

About the Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery and Procurement

The Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery and Procurement (MPBSDP) is one of many ministries under the Ontario Public Service (OPS). They're responsible for providing many of the digital, administrative, and procurement services that support the Ontario government and the public. The ministry works to improve how government services are delivered by modernizing technology systems, managing data securely, and supporting efficient operations across different ministries and agencies. Its work focuses on making government services more accessible, reliable, and efficient for both employees and residents throughout Ontario.

The Labour and Transportation Cluster (LTC) is a branch within the ministry that provides IT and digital support for the Ministry of Transportation (MTO) and the Ministry of Labour, Immigration, Training and Skills Development (MLITSD). LTC supports a wide range of technology-related services, including data analytics, application support, cybersecurity, and process automation. The cluster works closely with different teams and stakeholders to develop and maintain tools that help improve daily operations and decision-making.

Fun Facts

  • The Ontario Public Service (OPS) employs over 60,000 people across the province in areas such as technology, transportation, healthcare, finance, and public administration.
  • The Ministry of Transportation oversees more than 16,000 kilometres of provincial highways and over 2,800 bridges across Ontario.

Job Description - Data Reporting Analyst Co-op

As a Data Reporting Analyst, I got to work on a lot of different projects. One of the things I did was send out monthly reports to clients such as Rogers, Bell, and Peel Region. I generated and analyzed these reports using tools like SQL, Power BI, and Microsoft Excel. These reports played a crucial role in supporting our clients' operational decision-making processes. Working on these reports helped get me used to the different environments Ontario One Call uses and helped refine my skills in SQL, Power BI, and Microsoft Excel.

In addition, I spearheaded the development of a SharePoint site for internal clients. This SharePoint Site is used to access different tools like an analytics and reporting intake form, reporting schedules, data dictionary of terms, and other analytics resources. I incorporated best practices in web development and UI/UX principles that I've learned from my previous co-op at the University of Guelph. This project also introduced me to the world of project management and how a project workplan is executed from start to finish.

Goals

Develop Proficiency in Data Analysis and Reporting Tools

During this co-op work term, I significantly strengthened my data analysis skills by using tools like SQL and Power BI to generate impactful reports. This experience allowed me to refine my ability to analyze data in real-world applications. It was also my first opportunity to write and read code in a professional setting, offering valuable insights into how different elements come together to produce meaningful external reports. Creating these reports not only improved my technical skills but also enhanced my ability to interpret data and draw informed conclusions from the numbers.

Develop Foundational Skills in Machine Learning for Predictive Analysis

I had the opportunity to develop a predictive model leveraging machine learning to forecast the number of locate requests for upcoming years. This project provided me with a solid foundation in machine learning and predictive analytics while offering firsthand experience working on a project with the potential to shape future business decisions. Additionally, it allowed me to enhance my problem-solving skills by addressing challenges in data preprocessing, model optimization, and result interpretation to ensure the model’s accuracy and reliability

Develop Strong Communication and Collaboration Skills

Throughout my co-op term I improved my communication and collaboration skills. I attended daily standup meetings which gave me a lot of exposure to how other people communicate their ideas to the rest of the team. I also communicated with diverse stakeholders to gather requirements for a SharePoint site that centralized tools for internal teams. Working in an agile environment, I collaborated closely with cross-functional teams to design and implement the SharePoint site. This project honed my skills in giving me firsthand experience in project management and in planning and executing a work plan from start to finish.

Conclusion

Reflecting on my co-op experience at Ontario One Call, I'm proud of the skills I developed and the contributions I made.
