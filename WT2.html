About Ontario One Call

Ontario One Call is a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting safety and preventing damage to underground infrastructure across Ontario. As Ontario’s regulated one-call notification system, they act as the intermediary between underground infrastructure owners and excavators. Ontario One Call ensures a seamless connection between excavators and the owners of underground infrastructure like gas lines, water pipes, and telecommunications cables.

When an individual or organization submits a locate request, Ontario One Call notifies the relevant infrastructure owners, facilitating the process of marking underground utilities. This collaboration minimizes the risk of accidental damage, which could lead to service disruptions, costly repairs, or even serious safety hazards. Their mission is to reduce risks, protect essential services, and educate communities about the importance of safe digging practices.

Fun Facts

  • Ontario One Call processes more than 1,000,000 locate requests every year
  • Ontario law requires you to contact Ontario One Call before starting any excavation project, even planting a tree in your backyard
  • From utilities to municipalities, over 850 organizations rely on Ontario One Call to protect their underground infrastructure

Job Description - Data Reporting Analyst Co-op

As a Data Reporting Analyst, I got to work on a lot of different projects. One of the things I did was send out monthly reports to clients such as Rogers, Bell, and Peel Region. I generated and analyzed these reports using tools like SQL, Power BI, and Microsoft Excel. These reports played a crucial role in supporting our clients' operational decision-making processes. Working on these reports helped get me used to the different environments Ontario One Call uses and helped refine my skills in SQL, Power BI, and Microsoft Excel.

In addition, I spearheaded the development of a SharePoint site for internal clients. This SharePoint Site is used to access different tools like an analytics and reporting intake form, reporting schedules, data dictionary of terms, and other analytics resources. I incorporated best practices in web development and UI/UX principles that I've learned from my previous co-op at the University of Guelph. This project also introduced me to the world of project management and how a project workplan is executed from start to finish.
